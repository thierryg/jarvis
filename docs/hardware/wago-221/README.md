## Key points for the project

Source: 221-412 sheet, version 11.03.2024 (values identical for the whole 221 series in the 4 mm² size).

- Accepted cross-sections: **solid wire 0.2 to 4 mm² (24 to 12 AWG)**, **fine-stranded 0.14 to 4 mm² (24 to 12 AWG)**, stranded 0.2 to 4 mm². Nominal cross-section 4 mm² / 12 AWG.
- **Strip length: 11 mm**.
- EN 60664 ratings: **450 V / 32 A** (overvoltage category II, pollution degree 2). Per UL 486C: 600 V / 20 A.
- Maximum ambient temperature 85 °C (T85). Transparent polycarbonate housing, UL94 V2.
- The AWG 22 wires (about 0.33 mm²) specified by the Novomatic 200 manual are within the accepted range (0.2 mm² minimum for solid, 0.14 mm² for fine-stranded).
